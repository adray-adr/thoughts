This is what a full post looks like. It has a title, an excerpt for the feed preview, and as much room as you need.

## Two kinds of entries

- **Notes** live in `src/content/notes/` — just a date and a body, no title needed.
- **Posts** live in `src/content/posts/` — title, excerpt, tags, and full formatting.

Both show up together on the homepage, sorted by date, so the feed reads like one continuous stream of thought even though the writing formats differ.
